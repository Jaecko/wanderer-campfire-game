# Wanderer Campfire Game

A little idle game by the fire, fed by your journey through Azeroth. Made for World of Warcraft Forever.
An extension of [Wanderer](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer): it needs Wanderer 1.4 or later.

- A campfire in a tall window: stoke it, buy its producers and upgrades; it burns while you are away. Seven tiers, the seventh without end; a new fire for its ashes, stronger each time.
- Companions with faces, NPCs of the game in real game gear, with roles, traits, talents and three rarities, set out on real-time expeditions across Azeroth (forty-four zones, twenty-three dungeons, seven raids).
- Threats and counters with real spells, the best party in one click, contracts on the board, a standing order to set out again on each return.
- One card when you come back, reports that tell the story of the road, a rested camp after a long absence; seen contracts and last week's chest wait for you.
- Your real journey feeds the camp: exploration opens zones, your level opens tiers and dungeons, your professions, quests, fish, dishes and the bosses you kill make it stronger.
- It gives nothing in the real game and runs nothing while its window is closed but one timer for the next return. Opened from Wanderer's menu, a key of yours or /wcampfire.

Download: [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/wanderer-campfire-game). More on [wanderer-addon.com](https://wanderer-addon.com).
