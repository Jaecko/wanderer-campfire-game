# Wanderer Campfire Game

A little idle game by the fire, fed by your journey through Azeroth. Made for World of Warcraft Forever.
An extension of [Wanderer](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer): it needs Wanderer 0.43 or later.

- A campfire grows from a few twigs to an outpost: six tiers, six buildings, four resources.
- Companions in the colors of WoW's classes, with roles, traits, talents and forged gear, set out on expeditions across Azeroth in real time (forty zones, nineteen dungeons, seven raids).
- Your real journey feeds the camp: exploration achievements open zones, your level opens tiers and dungeons, your professions strengthen it and fill the quartermaster's orders, the dungeons you clear bring more for good.
- Fireside events with choices, incidents on the road, trophies, feats, and a new start from the outpost.
- It gives nothing in the real game and runs nothing while its window is closed. Opened from Wanderer's menu, a key of yours or /wcampfire.

Download: [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/wanderer-campfire-game). More on [wanderer-addon.com](https://wanderer-addon.com).
